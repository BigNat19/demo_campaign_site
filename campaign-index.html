About

Dara grew up three blocks from the ward office on Linden Street and still lives there today. After a decade running the neighborhood's tenant resource desk, Dara has sat across the table from landlords, city planners, and families who couldn't make rent — and learned exactly where the ward's systems break down.

This campaign started with a simple frustration: decisions about this ward keep getting made in rooms residents never hear about. Dara is running to move those decisions back into the open, block by block.

"I'm not interested in a council seat as a title. I want the seat because I know which potholes never get filled and which ones do — and I know why." Dara Whitfield

Issues

Four things Ward 4 keeps asking for and hasn't gotten.

Housing costs

Push for a ward-level rent registry so residents can see what similar units actually rent for, and back a stronger tenant right-to-counsel program funded from the existing legal aid line item.

Bus reliability

The 14 and 22 routes miss their posted times more than a third of the time. Dara will push transit to publish real arrival data for this ward and fund signal priority at the five worst intersections.

Budget transparency

Ward discretionary funds should be tracked on a public page, updated monthly, not buried in a year-end report. Residents should be able to see where every dollar of the ward's budget goes.

Park maintenance

Linden and Harbor Parks have been on the repair list for three years. Dara will move both into the next capital cycle and set up a resident maintenance council to flag problems before they pile up.
